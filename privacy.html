Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy covers "Hermes," a personal assistant application operated by Liam Halloran for his own use. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the account owner's explicit consent through Google sign-in, Hermes may access Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts) only for the Google accounts that authorized it.

How data is used

Data is used solely to perform tasks requested by the account owner, such as reading or drafting email, checking or adding calendar events, and working with documents. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored on a private, self-hosted server controlled by the owner. Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
